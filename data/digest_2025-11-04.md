# ⚡ **Grayskull: A Featherweight Computer Vision Library for Embedded Systems**  

Imagine adding computer vision capabilities to a tiny microcontroller without drowning in dependencies—**Grayskull** makes it real.  

### 🚀 **Why Grayskull?**  
- **No bloated dependencies** – Just a single **250-line C99 header file** designed for microcontrollers, drones, and robotics.  
- **Zero dynamic memory allocation** – Perfect for constrained environments.  
- **Minimalist yet powerful** – Supports blurring, edge detection, thresholding, FAST/ORB keypoints, and even face detection with LBP cascades.  

### ✨ **Key Features**  
✔ **Image processing** – Crop, resize, blur, Sobel edge detection, Otsu thresholding.  
✔ **Object tracking** – FAST/ORB keypoints & descriptors.  
✔ **Morphological ops** – Erosion, dilation, connected components.  
✔ **LBP cascades** – Detect faces or objects with pre-trained models.  
✔ **PGM image I/O** – For easy debugging and testing.  

### 🛠 **Quick Example: Edge Detection in 5 Lines**  
```c  
#include "grayskull.h"  
struct gs_image img = gs_read_pgm("input.pgm");  
struct gs_image edges = gs_alloc(img.w, img.h);  
gs_sobel(edges, img);  
gs_write_pgm(edges, "output.pgm");  
```

### 🔥 **Ideal For:**

- **Robotics & drones**
