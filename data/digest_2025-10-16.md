# **Hacker News Daily Digest: Gemini 3.0 Leak Spotted in the Wild?**

The AI community has been buzzing with whispers that **Google's Gemini 3.0 might be stealth-testing in Google AI Studio**, and one user may have accidentally tapped into it—via an Xbox controller.  

### **The Telltale SVG Test**  
User reports suggest Gemini 3.0 is currently undergoing **A/B testing**, and the smoking gun? **High-quality SVG generation**, a surprisingly effective benchmark for judging model performance (as demonstrated by @simonw’s famous "pelican riding a bicycle" test).  

When asked to generate an SVG of an **Xbox 360 controller**, one tester got wildly superior results compared to **Gemini 2.5 Pro**. The output was **cleaner, more detailed, and far more accurate**—all while maintaining proper Markdown formatting.  

### **What Gives It Away?**  
- **Model ID:** The purported "Gemini 3.0" test version carried the cryptic identifier **`ecpt50a2y6mpgkcn`**.  
- **Performance Metrics:** The model had roughly **24 seconds higher Time-To-First-Token (TTFT)** and **40% longer outputs**—hinting at **deeper reasoning** but not necessarily a "GPT-5 Pro" level of compute.  

### **What Does This Mean?**  
If true, this could signal that **Gemini 3.0 Pro (not Flash) is being tested against 2.5 Pro**—which would make sense, as comparisons between **2.5 Pro and 3.0 Flash** would be less meaningful.  

For AI enthusiasts, this is **another piece of evidence suggesting Google is ramping up for a major release**. Could the full **Gemini 3.0** reveal be around the corner?
