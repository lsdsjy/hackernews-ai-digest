# Israeli Government’s Secret “Wink” System: Google and Amazon’s $1.2B Cloud Deal Involves Coded Alerts  

**In a bombshell investigation**, The Guardian, +972 Magazine, and Local Call have uncovered a clandestine arrangement between Israel, Google, and Amazon—dubbed the **“winking mechanism”**—designed to secretly notify Israel whenever foreign governments demand access to its data.  

### **The Secret Code to Bypass Legal Orders**  
- As part of a **$1.2 billion cloud-computing contract (Project Nimbus)**, Israel required Google and Amazon to use **hidden signals in payments** to alert the government whenever they were forced to hand over Israeli data to foreign law enforcement.  
- Typically, tech companies like Google and Amazon comply with legal data requests while often being **gagged from informing customers**. Israel, concerned about losing control of sensitive data, **invented this covert workaround**.  

### **Extraordinary Contract Terms**  
The deal, signed in 2021, includes strict conditions:  
- **No shutdown clause**: Google and Amazon **cannot suspend services** for any Israeli agency—**even if they violate human rights policies**, unlike Microsoft (which recently cut off Israel’s military over surveillance concerns).  
- **No transparency**: Both companies **cannot disclose how Israel uses their tech**, despite concerns over military occupation and potential war crimes in Gaza.  
- **Microsoft refused** similar demands, potentially costing it the contract.  

### **Implications for Surveillance & War**  
The Israeli military heavily relies on cloud infrastructure for **mass data analysis**, including **intercepted Palestinian communications**. Recently, intelligence sources suggest Israel planned to move **spy data from Microsoft to Amazon’s servers**.  

### **Tech Giants Deny Wrongdoing**  
- Amazon: Claims no visibility into customer workloads.  
- Google: **Denies evading legal obligations** but avoids specifics.  

### **Why This Matters**  
This deal raises grave ethical questions:  
- Are Google and Amazon **complicit in enabling surveillance** or military operations?  
- Should **corporate interests outweigh human rights concerns**?  
- A **UN commission has accused Israel of genocide** in Gaza—**should tech firms fuel its military infrastructure?**
