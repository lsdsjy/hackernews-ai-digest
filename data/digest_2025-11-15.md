### Things that aren't doing the thing

#### [Submission URL](https://strangestloop.io/essays/things-that-arent-doing-the-thing) | 389 points | by [downboots](https://news.ycombinator.com/user?id=downboots) | [188 comments](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45939431)

### **Stop Planning. Start Doing.** 🚀  

We’ve all been there: endlessly preparing, organizing, researching, and talking about *the thing*—instead of actually doing it.  

- ✅ Preparing to do the thing? Not the thing.  
- ✅ Scheduling time for the thing? Still not the thing.  
- ✅ Writing a to-do list, tweeting about it, or reading how others did it? Nope. Not the thing.  
- ✅ Beating yourself up about not doing it? Still. Not. The. Thing.  

The only thing that counts is **doing the damn thing.**  

So close this tab. Stop planning. And go do it. 🚀
