### Leaked Apple M5 9 core Geekbench scores

#### [Submission URL](https://browser.geekbench.com/v6/cpu/14173685) | 307 points | by [aurareturn](https://news.ycombinator.com/user?id=aurareturn) | [499 comments](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45427197)

**M4 iPad Pro Shatters Performance Records in Geekbench 6.5.0**  

The next-gen **iPad17,3** (likely the **M4-powered iPad Pro**) has appeared in Geekbench 6.5.0, posting staggering **4,133 (Single-Core) and 15,437 (Multi-Core) scores**, showcasing Apple’s continued dominance in mobile silicon.  

### **Key Specs & Performance Breakdown**  
- **CPU:** ARM-based 9-core design (1+3+6 cluster) running at **4.42 GHz**  
- **Memory:** **11.2GB** (possibly a base variant)  
- **Benchmark Highlights:**  
  - **Single-Core:** Nearly **2x faster** than M1 iPads (M1: ~2,300)  
  - **Multi-Core:** **Approaching MacBook Pro M3 territory** (M3 MacBook Pro: ~12,000 in GB6)  
  - **AI/ML Tasks:** Crushing numbers in **Object Detection (6,032), Object Remover (5,276, 1.23 Gpixels/sec), and HDR processing (4,678)**  

### **What This Means**  
- **Desktop-Class Workflows:** On par with many Intel/AMD laptops, making the iPad Pro even more viable for pro apps.  
- **AI Acceleration:** Massive leaps in **machine learning tasks** (SME-i8i32, SME2 extensions).  
- **iOS 26 Hint?** The benchmark lists **iOS 26.0**, suggesting an early test build.  

### **How It Compares**  
- **MacBook Air M3 (GB6):** ~3,000 SC / ~12,000 MC  
- **Snapdragon X Elite (rumored):** ~3,000 SC / ~14,000 MC  
- **Intel Core i9-14900K:** ~3,200 SC / ~20,000 MC (but at much higher power draw)  

**The Verdict:** Apple’s M4 is shaping up to be a **monster**—anyone still questioning the iPad as a "pro" device?
