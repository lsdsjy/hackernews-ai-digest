### **Mission to Save the Moon? NASA Explores Options to Stop Asteroid 2024 YR4**  

A newly discovered asteroid, **2024 YR4**, once had a **3% chance of hitting Earth** in December 2032—but those fears were quickly ruled out. Now, scientists are worried about something else: **a possible lunar impact** that could unleash dangerous micrometeoroid debris in Earth's orbit, threatening satellites and even astronauts.  

#### **Key Details:**  
- **Asteroid Size:** ~60 meters in diameter (roughly half the size of the Chelyabinsk meteor).  
- **Lunar Impact Risk:** ~4% chance in December 2032.  
- **Threat Level:** A strike on the Moon could create ejecta that **increases space debris in Earth’s orbit by 1,000x for several days.**  

#### **Mission Options on the Table:**  
1. **Reconnaissance (Late 2028 Launch)**
   - Flyby or rendezvous missions to study the asteroid.
   - Could help confirm trajectory and composition.  

2. **Deflection (Not Feasible)**
   - Too difficult—likely requires too much energy.  

3. **"Robust Disruption" (Blowing It Up)**
   - **Kinetic Impactors (2029-2032 Launch):** Crashing a spacecraft into it.  
   - **Nuclear Option (2029-2031 Launch):** Breaking it apart before it reaches the Moon.  

#### **The Clock is Ticking**  
If a mission is greenlit, we'd need to act fast—developing and launching a spacecraft in about **3 years**. Even if the Moon strike is ruled out later, studying this asteroid could still be valuable for planetary defense.  

This scenario highlights the growing need for asteroid mitigation strategies—because sometimes, even a **near-miss** can be too close for comfort.
